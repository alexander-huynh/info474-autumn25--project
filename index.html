Not All Cars Pollute Equally
Every car produces CO₂, but not all of them pollute the same. Some barely sip fuel, others burn through it fast. This walkthrough breaks down why those differences exist—and where your own car fits in.
Step 1 — Fuel Types: Where Emissions Begin
First stop: fuel. Petrol and diesel don’t burn the same, and it shows in their emissions. This section shows how CO₂ output shifts depending on what goes into the tank.
Step 2 — Engine Size: Bigger Engines, Bigger Emissions
Engine size matters. Bigger displacement typically means more CO₂. This section lets you explore the trend and see where the outliers land.
Step 3 — Horsepower: Performance vs. Environmental Cost
More power usually means more emissions. This view connects horsepower to CO₂ so you can spot which performance cars stay efficient and which ones trade power for pollution.
Step 4 — Manufacturer Personality: Who Builds Clean, Who Doesn’t?
Different brands have different tendencies. Some consistently build lower-emission cars, while others lean the opposite way. This chart shows where each manufacturer stands on average.
Step 5 — Find Your Balance: Performance, Price, and CO₂
Mix and match the factors yourself. Adjust CO₂, horsepower, price range, and fuel type, and see which cars best match your personal priorities.
Step 6 — Search Your Own Car: Where Do You Stand?
Curious how your current car compares? Type it in and see how it stacks up against thousands of other models in the dataset.
What the Data Says About “Normal” Cars
Stepping back, you’ll notice patterns: fuel, engine size, horsepower, and brand strategy all influence emissions. Most cars cluster in the middle, but you’ve now seen what pushes them higher or lower.
About This Dataset
These visuals use EU vehicle registration + emissions data, along with specs like engine size, horsepower, fuel type, and price. Values are simplified for clarity—they’re for exploration, not regulation.
Where You Go From Here
This walkthrough isn’t here to tell you what to buy. It’s here to make the climate cost visible. Take the insights with you, whether you’re car shopping or just looking at the road differently.
